# [High] Single-use coupon reusable on POST /api/v3/bookings
**Severity:** High (7.0) — base 6.5 (coupon_reuse); asset criticality 1.2 (+0.5)
**Owner:** booking-svc (#booking-eng)  **Status:** OPEN  **OWASP:** API6:2023 Unrestricted Business Flow  **CWE:** CWE-837


## Business impact
Direct revenue loss / financial integrity break in the booking flow; exploitable by any logged-in agent.

## Proof (oracle: `business_rule`)
Single-use coupon WELCOME10 applied twice by a2_admin: totals 3600.0 and 3600.0 (quotes 4000.0, 4000.0)

```bash
curl -s -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:8100/api/v3/bookings' -H 'Authorization: Bearer $A2_ADMIN_TOKEN' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"quote_id": "<QUOTE_ID>", "coupon": "WELCOME10"}'
curl -s -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:8100/api/v3/bookings' -H 'Authorization: Bearer $A2_ADMIN_TOKEN' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"quote_id": "<QUOTE_ID>", "coupon": "WELCOME10"}'
```
Evidence audit IDs: 910, 917 — replay with `python -m tripwire replay <audit_id>`


## Suggested fix
Record coupon redemption atomically (unique constraint on coupon+tenant) and check before applying.
